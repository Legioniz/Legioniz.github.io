## Smart Electrical Monitoring

- Category: Data and systems / energy analytics demonstration
- Status: Synthetic-data demonstration
- Summary: Local-only workflow for organizing electricity readings, visualizing usage, exploring anomalies and forecasts, and estimating appliance consumption.
- Technologies: Python, SQL/MySQL, web interface, analytics.
- Repository: https://github.com/Legioniz/Smart_Electrical_Monitoring_2.13
- Scope note: Uses synthetic electricity data. It is not connected to Meralco, a real smart meter, a utility billing system, or customer energy accounts.
